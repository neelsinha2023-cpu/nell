## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-science nutraceutical efficacy grounded in holistic botanical alchemy. Rooted in the concept of *Ojas*—the primordial essence of vitality, energy, and radiant immunity—the visual language balances clinical precision with organic warmth.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Glassmorphism and Tactile Depth**:
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke holistic apothecary heritage, framed by hyper-disciplined geometric layouts and generous breathing room.
- **Botanical Vitality:** Luminescent chlorophyll greens intertwine with warm solar golds, set against warm alabaster stone backdrops and deep carbon anchors.
- **Dimensional Tactility:** Translucent frosted card containers, softly diffused ambient drops, and delicate warm-light borders produce the sensation of looking through apothecary glass illuminated by morning sun.
- **Audience & Emotion:** Designed for health-conscious consumers and longevity optimizers seeking premium, pure, science-backed wellness formulas. The experience radiates calm confidence, purity, regenerative energy, and quiet luxury.
